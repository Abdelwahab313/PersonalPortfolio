About

I'm a senior software engineer based in Cairo. For eight years I've built and operated real products end to end, with backend and platform as my home ground.

Most of my work is the unglamorous kind that keeps a product standing: keeping a database out of CPU saturation, replacing autoscaling that killed long-running jobs, and making an LLM engine fail over cleanly when a provider degrades. I like owning a system from the architecture decision to the incident at 3am.

I've worked on a meal subscription platform serving 100K daily users, a US health product, and the AI translation platform behind WPML.
